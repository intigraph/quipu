## Suggested paragraph

Recent work addresses complementary parts of agent memory. Vishisht Choudhary's
[*Keep Your Receipts*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17719) studies cleanup after source
withdrawal, using evidence receipts and re-derivation audits. Akash Raj's
[*Plant Memory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19131) describes reviewed maintenance
knowledge with evidence-bearing facts and MCP access, evaluated on synthetic work
orders. Quipu's contribution here is a governed RDF store and shape-aware
reconciliation of divergent graphs. These are related problems rather than a
head-to-head evaluation; the cited abstracts do not establish comparative temporal
or withdrawal behavior.
